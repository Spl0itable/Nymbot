    Anonymous chat

    Your chat is already end-to-end encrypted, but Nymbot still sees which pubkey is talking to it. Turn this on and every message, and every reply, travels under a throwaway key generated on this device. Credits move across as blind vouchers Nymbot signs without seeing, so its own records cannot link the two.

    Turn it on to fund the throwaway key, by hand or automatically.

    When the throwaway key runs low, move credits across without asking. Same blind vouchers, same unlinkability — it just saves doing it by hand before every chat.

    Spends that many credits from your nym and mints the same value onto the throwaway key. Nymbot signs the vouchers blind, so it cannot match what it spent to what it later accepts.
